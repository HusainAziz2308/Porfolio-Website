GRAPHIC DESIGN

SAINTEK EVENT POSTER

DESIGN GOAL

The objective was to create a bold, dynamic, and informative poster design for the annual Saintek event. The design needed to immediately grab attention while clearly conveying the event's core dates and themes.

Download High-Res

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

  • Visual Hierarchy
  • Typography Focus
  • Color Theory
  • Adobe Suite (Ps/Ai)

COLOR PALETTE & TYPEFACE

We used a high-energy color palette dominated by deep blues and neon accents to represent technological advancement. A strong, geometric typeface was chosen to ensure maximum legibility and impact in print and digital media.
